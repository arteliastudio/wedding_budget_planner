How to Use
A quick guide to everything in your Wedding Budget Tracker.
Before you start: This tracker saves your data to your browser automatically. For safety, download a backup regularly using the Download Backup button in Settings. If you clear your browser data or switch browsers, your data will not transfer automatically.
1
Set Up Your Wedding Details
Tap Settings in the navigation bar. Enter the couple's names, wedding date, and your total budget. Pick your favorite theme color from the swatches or use the color wheel to choose any custom color. Hit Save Changes when done. Your names and countdown will appear in the header.
2
Track Your Expenses on the Dashboard
The Dashboard is your main workspace. Click any category card to expand it. Fill in: Estimated (your budgeted amount), Actual (what you've been quoted or paid), Deposit Paid (any partial payment made), and Payment Due Date (when the remaining balance is owed). The Balance Due calculates automatically.
3
Your 5 Summary Cards
At the top of the Dashboard you'll see two groups of cards. Budget Totals shows your Total Budget, Total Spent so far, and what's Remaining. Estimates shows your Total Estimated cost across all categories and your Estimated Remaining (how much of your estimates you haven't spent yet). These update instantly as you fill in numbers.
4
Watch Your Due Date Countdowns
Once you enter a payment due date on a category, that card will show a countdown. Head to the Due Dates tab for a full list of all upcoming payments in one place, with a total balance due at the top so you know exactly how much is coming up.
5
Mark Categories as Paid
Once a vendor is fully paid, tap the Mark Paid button on their card. The border turns green and you'll hear a confirmation chime. Paid categories still appear on the dashboard but are visually distinguished from unpaid ones.
6
Budget Breakdown Tab
The Budget Breakdown tab shows a visual bar chart. The full bar always represents 100% of your total budget. Each colored segment (in shades of your theme color) shows what portion of the budget each category takes. A lighter segment shows your unspent budget. There are separate bars for Estimated and Actual spending.
7
Customize Your Categories
Go to Edit Categories to add new expense types, rename existing ones, or remove anything you don't need. Always click Save Categories when you're done to lock in your changes.
8
Export & Backup Your Data
In Settings, tap Print / Export to PDF to save or print a clean summary of your budget. Use Download Backup to save all your data as a file you can keep or share. Use Import Backup to restore that data on any device. We strongly recommend downloading a backup regularly so your data is always safe.
9
Auto-Save
Every change you make saves automatically to your browser. No account needed, no internet required. Your data stays on your device and is always there when you come back. Remember to download a backup from Settings to keep your data safe.
Important Notes
Please read these before you start using your tracker.
1
Clearing Browser History or Cache Deletes Your Data
Don't clear your browser's cache, cookies, or history. If you do, all your file data will be deleted too.
2
Download Backups Regularly
Your backup is your safety net. After you finish planning each section, go to Settings and tap Download Backup. Save the file to your Desktop or Google Drive. If something goes wrong, you can use Import Backup to restore your data.
3
Data Lives Locally on Your Device
All your data is stored only in your browser on your device.
4
Always Download a Backup Before Clearing History
If you need to clear your browser history, download a backup file first. This way, if your tracker data gets accidentally deleted, you can restore it using the backup.
5
Never Use Private or Incognito Mode
Never open this file in a private or incognito window. If you close that window, all your data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
6
Don't Save Tracker Files in Cloud Storage
Don't save the files in Google Drive, iCloud, or other cloud storage. The file data is stored in your browser on your device, not in cloud files. If you try to use the tracker from cloud storage, it won't work.
7
Reinstalling Your Browser Erases Everything
If you reinstall your browser or reset it to factory settings, all your file data will be deleted.
8
Don't Switch Browsers
Chrome, Safari, and Firefox each save data separately. If you open the tracker in a different browser, you won't see your old data unless you import a backup you downloaded from your first browser.
9
Chrome Profiles Have Separate Storage
If you use multiple profiles in Chrome (personal, work, etc.), each profile has its own data. Your data won't appear in another profile unless you import a backup.
10
Cleanup Apps Will Erase Your Data
Apps like CleanMyMac and CCleaner delete everything, including your tracker data. This is the most common reason people lose their data!
11
Turn Off Browser Auto-Clear Settings
Some browsers automatically delete all site data when you close the browser. If this is on, your tracker will reset every time you close the browser. Turn this off in your browser's privacy settings.
12
Data Doesn't Sync Between Devices
Your data on your iPhone won't automatically appear on your Mac or other devices. To move your data between devices, download a backup file on one device and import it on the other.
13
Be Cautious on Shared Computers
If you use a shared family computer or work computer, be careful. Someone else might clear the browser data, or your workplace might automatically delete it. Keep a backup copy saved somewhere only you can access.
